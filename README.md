> Discover the serenity and beauty of Persian culture through a peaceful journey inside an authentic Iranian garden.

Whispers of the Garden is a calm, reflective experience — explore a hand-drawn world, uncover poetry from masters like **Hafez** and **Rumi**, and listen to original **Santur** music. Less a game, more a moment of stillness.

## ✨ Features

- 🌿 A hand-drawn, explorable Persian garden
- 📜 Curated couplets from Hafez, Rumi and other masters
- 🎶 Original Santur synthesis &amp; generative ambient audio
- ✨ Petal &amp; particle effects built with SwiftUI `Canvas`
- 🔒 Fully offline — no accounts, no tracking

## 🛠️ Built with

- **Swift** &amp; **SwiftUI**
- Custom audio synthesis — `SanturSynthesizer`, `SFXGenerator`, `GardenAudioEngine`
- SwiftUI `Canvas` particle systems
- iOS 16+
