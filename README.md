# Whispers of the Garden

A small iOS app about Persian poetry, set in a hand-drawn garden. You wander around and uncover couplets from Hafez, Rumi and a few others, with santur music playing underneath. It's slow and quiet, more something to sit with than a game to play.

Every bit of art in it (the garden, the plants, all the elements) I drew by hand in **Procreate**. That illustration work is really the heart of the app.

On the App Store: https://apps.apple.com/it/app/whispers-of-the-garden/id6760307230

Built in SwiftUI. The santur and the sound effects are synthesized in code rather than recorded, and the petals and particles are drawn with `Canvas`. It runs entirely offline and doesn't collect anything (there's a privacy policy in the repo).

Needs iOS 16 or later.
